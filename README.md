### 👨‍💻 About Me
Welcome to my harbor. I am a **Backend & Software Engineer** focusing on building robust, scalable applications within the .NET ecosystem. 

My journey began in data engineering and business intelligence, which gave me a profound appreciation for database optimization and enterprise architecture. Now, I dedicate my expertise to crafting high-performance backend systems, developing RESTful APIs, and solving complex business logic problems with C#, ASP.NET Core, and modern web technologies.
